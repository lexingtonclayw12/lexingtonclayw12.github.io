💧 The Water Cycle

7th Grade Science • Learn, flip cards, and test yourself!

1. How water moves

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the surface, underground, and the atmosphere. The Sun provides energy for evaporation; gravity pulls rain down and helps rivers flow.

Water can also soak into soil (infiltration), move underground, freeze, melt, or be released by plants (transpiration).

4. Try a science experiment

5. Explain it yourself

A drop starts in the Atlantic Ocean, becomes part of a cloud, falls as rain in North Carolina, and returns to the ocean in a river. Explain its journey using at least four vocabulary words.

The Sun heats ocean water, causing evaporation. Water vapor cools and undergoes condensation, forming droplets in clouds. Water falls as precipitation, flows downhill as runoff into rivers, and returns to the ocean for collection.
