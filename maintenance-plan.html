Website Maintenance Plan

What’s included

One plan, kept running behind the scenes so you never have to think about it:

  • Hosting, security patches, and backups — the site never goes stale or gets hacked
  • Uptime monitoring, with a same-day alert the moment anything breaks
  • Up to 30 minutes of edits a month — hours, photos, a promo, an announcement
  • A SiteScore re-check every quarter, with a short report on anything slipping
  • Same-week turnaround on requests

Price

$129/month. Billed monthly, no contract, cancel any time.

Why $129

This isn’t priced against how long an edit takes us. It’s priced against what a stale or broken site costs you — one missed call from a customer who hit a dead link is worth more than a year of this.

You’re never locked in

Whether or not you ever sign up for this, the site and the domain are yours the day the build finishes. That promise doesn’t change with or without a plan — Wright Click Studios doesn’t hold a website hostage.

Need more than the monthly 30 minutes?

Bigger requests — a new page, a redesigned section, extra photography — are billed per-project at a fixed quote up front, the same way the original build was. No surprise hourly rate.

How billing works

Handled securely through Stripe. You’ll get a receipt every month, and you can cancel any time with a quick email or phone call — no cancellation fee, no questions asked.
