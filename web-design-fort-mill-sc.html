The Situation

Why it matters
here specifically.

Fort Mill has a particular problem: it grew fast, and a large share of the people searching for what you do moved here in the last few years. They have no idea who the good barber is. They have no neighbour to ask. They open a phone, type something with "near me" on the end of it, and pick from what comes back.

That search result is now your front door, and it is being answered by whoever looks most established — not by whoever does the best work. If your site is a Facebook page, an out-of-date template, or nothing at all, you are not in the running for a customer who would have loved you.

We fix that with one hand-built site, a Google Business Profile that actually matches it, and copy that says which town you are in — because "serving the greater area" tells Google nothing at all.
