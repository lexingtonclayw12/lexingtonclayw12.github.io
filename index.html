What Changed
A Room, Not A Page

Deep espresso and warm ivory — it feels like walking into a recital hall instead of a school gym. Calm, uncluttered, and expensive-looking without shouting.

Real Brass, Not Gold Paint

An aged brass tone on the buttons, the thin dividing lines, and the piano keys across the middle. It reads like a real metal finish, not a cheap gold gradient.

Lettering With A Voice

An elegant italic for the headline and a clean, plain typeface for everything else — so your eye knows instantly what matters most on the page.

Booking Never More Than A Tap Away

A "book a lesson" button before you scroll, and again everywhere you stop. Proof they're established sits right up top, where a nervous first-timer will see it.
